The versions of OEIS A062714 and A136094 retrieved during this review list exact lengths and representative sequences only through n = 9. A351468 records Newey’s general construction. We evaluated the cited bounds of Zălinescu, Radomirović and Tan under their stated hypotheses; the smallest resulting bounds for n = 11, …, 15 are respectively 102, 123, 145, 170, 197. Targeted web and arXiv searches did not identify earlier sequences as short as the five witnesses given here. These checks establish improvements over the inspected constructions, but do not establish first discovery or exclude unindexed or unpublished work.
