Internal checks made so far were carried out by separate AI agent instances, not by people, and are no substitute for review by a person. Two agents that were told not to read the checkers shipped here wrote their own programs and re-verified the witnesses as printed in §3. One used the subset recurrence in Python, validated against exhaustive enumeration on random small cases. The other, in C, enumerated all n! permutations for n = 11, 12, 13 and, for all five n, counted the permutations that are subsequences with a different dynamic programme, obtaining exactly n! each time. Both found that every single-term deletion destroys completeness. A third agent reproduced `run_all.sh` byte for byte from a clean copy and ran negative tests on damaged files; a fourth checked the OEIS material against the OEIS style sheet and the OEIS rules on the use of AI.
